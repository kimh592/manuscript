# Project 1: *Manuscript*

Created for Typography & Interaction (’26–’27) under the MPS Communication Design program at The New School—this project attempts to illustrate the article “Fuck All” by Kenneth FitzGerald through the form of a handmade website. It includes the original article and a personal response.

## **Fuck All vs Fuck Content**s

This article was a direct response to Michael Rock’s “Fuck Content,” in which Rock argues that design’s true ‘content’ lies in its form, treatments, and storytelling… rather than the subject/story itself. On the other hand, FitzGerald argues otherwise. He warns us that such a dismissal of the subject may cause the public to forget that design can contain political, cultural, and personal opinions.

## **Inspiration Behind the Design**

In the article, FitzGerald talks about Rock’s comparison between Jan Tschichold’s Die Neue Typographie (1928) and Nike’s retail campaign. From Rock’s point of view, those two design moments are equally significant—in other words, suggesting a blurring of the line between commercial design and design driven by ideals.

This part of the writing became a big inspiration behind the design of this project. Borrowing elements from the cover design of Tschichold’s Die Neue Typographie, the project is set against a vibrant, warm yellow background with black divider lines and orange-red highlights. The asymmetrical layout was translated into the h1, h3, and paragraph text each occupying their own scrolling sections, creating a layout where the elements move independently but still work together as one system.
